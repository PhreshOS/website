Most web apps live and die with a tab. Close it by accident and the work inside
it stops; open the same app on another device and you get a second, separate
copy that knows nothing about the first.

PhreshOS is an open-source operating system for programs built with web
technology, running on your own machine. Think of it as a garden: your programs
are what grows in it, and the browser is a window in the garden wall.

## The short answer

Close the window and the garden does not stop growing. Whatever your programs do
in the background goes on, and when you look again, every window is where you
left it.

Closing a program's *window* is different: it ends that program's run, the way
closing an app does on any desktop. Closing the *browser* only closes your view.

## One System, many screens

A garden can have more than one window. Open PhreshOS in two browsers, or on two
screens, and both show the same programs and the same windows, because there is
only one of each: the System's. Move a window in one and it moves in the other;
minimize it and it is minimized in both. Nothing is copied or synced after the
fact.

## Each screen looks from its own place

What the screens share is what lives in the garden. How each one looks at it is
its own:

- **Where it looks.** On PhreshOS, windows live on a field larger than the
  screen, and each screen can look at a different part of it. One can stay on
  your project while the other watches the view where your agent is working.
- **How it looks.** Light or dark, and how large everything is, belong to each
  browser. The System's own colors, set in Appearance, are the same everywhere.

The System keeps none of this. It knows where each window is, not who is looking
or from where.

## Your agent does not need a screen

An AI agent reaches the same programs from outside, through the `phresh`
command line or the SDK, and uses what those programs were built to offer. It
works whether or not a browser is open. A screen is for you, when you want to
watch.

## Plant it

PhreshOS is free and open source.

```sh
npm install --global @phreshos/cli
phresh system install
```
