title: Alert
description: A message that stays in place, such as a failed save.
---

<AlertShowcase />

`Alert` shows a message where it applies. Its `color` is its meaning:

```tsx
<Alert color="danger" title="Could not save">
  The System refused the change. Try again in a moment.
</Alert>
```

It is painted in the `subtle` level of its color, the same level as a
selection, and leads with the icon of its meaning: a circle for `danger`, a
triangle for `warning`, a check for `success`, and an `i` for anything else.
`icon` replaces it, and `icon={false}` leaves the Alert without one.

`warning` and `danger` Alerts are announced as they appear; others are read as
status. The Alert keeps the Appearance radius, like any region.

## Props

| Prop | Default | Sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `"info"` | Its meaning and paint |
| `title` | — | A short summary above the message |
| `icon` | The icon of its meaning | Its icon, or `false` for none |
| Surface props | — | Radius, material, and native attributes |
