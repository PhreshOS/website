`Fieldset` groups related fields under a title, such as one section of
settings:

```tsx
<Fieldset title="Layout" description="Shared spacing and corners.">
  <NumberField label="Spacing" defaultValue={12} />
  <NumberField label="Radius" defaultValue={10} />
</Fieldset>
```

Each field keeps its own label. The Fieldset adds only its title and
description, which also name the group for assistive technology, and the
`large` spacing between its fields.

## Props

| Prop | Sets |
| --- | --- |
| `title` | Its name |
| `description` | A line under the title |
| `fieldset` attributes | Such as `disabled` or `form` |
