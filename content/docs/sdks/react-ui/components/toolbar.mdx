`Toolbar` groups related controls into one keyboard stop. Tab moves into and
out of the toolbar; the arrow keys move between its controls. The controls
keep their own look and actions.

```tsx
<Toolbar aria-label="Document">
  <Toolbar.Group aria-label="History">
    <Button>Undo</Button>
    <Button>Redo</Button>
  </Toolbar.Group>
  <Toolbar.Separator />
  <Button color="primary">Save</Button>
</Toolbar>
```

Name every toolbar with `aria-label`. `Toolbar.Group` groups a smaller set,
and can have its own name.

## Vertical toolbars

```tsx
<Toolbar aria-label="Alignment" orientation="vertical">
  <Button>Start</Button>
  <Button>Center</Button>
  <Button>End</Button>
</Toolbar>
```

A vertical toolbar uses the up and down arrows. Disabled controls are skipped.

## Spacing and separators

`gap` sets the space between items, and `Toolbar.Group` has its own `gap`.
`Toolbar.Separator` turns to match the toolbar: vertical in a horizontal
toolbar, and the other way round.

Toolbar paints nothing. Put it in a Window, Panel, or Surface to give it a
background.

## Parts

| Part | Props |
| --- | --- |
| `Toolbar` | `aria-label`, `orientation` (`"horizontal"`), `gap` (`"medium"`), `color` for separators (`"foreground"`) |
| `Toolbar.Group` | `aria-label`, `gap`, `disabled` |
| `Toolbar.Separator` | — |
