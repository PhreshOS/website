---
title: Color Slider
description: Adjust one channel of a color, such as hue or opacity.
---

<ColorSliderShowcase />

`ColorSlider` changes one `channel` of a color on a track painted with that
channel's range. Its value is the whole color, as a hex string:

```tsx
<ColorSlider label="Hue" channel="hue" colorSpace="hsl" value={color} onChange={setColor} />
<ColorSlider label="Opacity" channel="alpha" value={color} onChange={setColor} />
```

`colorSpace` chooses where the channel comes from: `"hsl"` or `"hsb"` for hue
and saturation, `"rgb"` for red, green, and blue. `alpha` works in any space.

Inside a [`ColorPicker`](/docs/sdks/react-ui/components/color-picker) it edits the
picker's color without its own `value`.

## Props

| Prop | Default | Sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `channel` | — | The channel it changes |
| `colorSpace` | The color's own | Where the channel comes from |
| `value`, `defaultValue`, `onChange`, `onChangeEnd` | — | Its value |
| `orientation` | `"horizontal"` | Its direction |
| `label`, `description` | — | Its text |
| `size`, `disabled` | — | Its size and state |
