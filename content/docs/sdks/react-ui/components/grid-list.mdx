`GridList` lays a collection out as a grid of cards. It fits as many columns
as its width allows, each card at least `itemWidth` wide:

```tsx
<GridList aria-label="Programs" selectionMode="multiple" value={chosen} onChange={setChosen}>
  <GridList.Section id="system">
    <GridList.Header>System</GridList.Header>
    <GridList.Item id="settings" textValue="Settings">…</GridList.Item>
    <GridList.Item id="terminal" textValue="Terminal">…</GridList.Item>
  </GridList.Section>
</GridList>
```

A card holds whatever it is given. It is a whole flat Surface with the
default material: in the `default` color at rest, and once chosen in the
selection color of the collection, with a check in its corner. The arrow keys
move between cards.

## Selection

Selection works as in [ListBox](/docs/sdks/react-ui/components/list-box):
`single` by default, `multiple` for several, and `none` for cards that are
only shown, such as the progress of work. `value` and `onChange` use the
cards' `id`s.

## Sections

A `GridList.Section` spans the grid and lays its own cards out in the same
columns, under a `GridList.Header`.

## Drag and drop

Cards can be dragged out and things dropped onto them, as in
[Table](/docs/sdks/react-ui/components/table#drag-and-drop): pass what
`useDragAndDrop` returns as `dragAndDropHooks`. A card a drag would drop onto
shows as chosen.

## Reference

| Part | Is |
| --- | --- |
| `GridList` | The grid. `selectionMode`, `value`, `onChange`, `itemWidth` (sixteen times the `spacing` by default), `color` |
| `GridList.Item` | One card. `id`, `textValue`, `disabled`, `color` |
| `GridList.Section` | A group of cards across the grid |
| `GridList.Header` | A Section's heading |
