<BadgeShowcase />

A Badge names a state. It is not a control: it cannot be pressed or focused.

```tsx
<Badge color="success" dot>running</Badge>
<Badge color="danger" dot>stopped</Badge>
<Badge>idle</Badge>
```

It is painted in the `subtle` level of its color, like a selection, with its
text in the `strong` level. `dot` adds a dot in the full color, for a live
state. Its text is one control step smaller than its `size`.

For labels a person can select or remove, use a
[TagGroup](/docs/sdks/react-ui/components/tag-group).

## Props

| Prop | Default | Sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `"foreground"`, neutral | Its color role |
| `dot` | `false` | A leading dot in the full color |
| `size` | `"medium"` | Its size |
